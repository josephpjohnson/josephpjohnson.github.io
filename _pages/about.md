I am a **National Post Doctoral Fellow** at [The Institute of Mathematical Sciences (IMSc)](https://imsc.res.in), Chennai, working in theoretical and observational cosmology.

### Research Interests
* **Late-Universe Cosmology & Interacting Dark Sector:** Field-fluid mappings, coupled quintessence-dark matter models, and disformal interactions.
* **Tests of General Relativity & Modified Gravity:** Large-scale structure signatures, equivalence principle tests with VLBI, and Horndeski theories.
* **Model-Independent Reconstruction & Data Analysis:** Gaussian process reconstruction of late-Universe expansion history, Boltzmann solvers (`CLASS`), and Bayesian MCMC parameter inference (`Cobaya`).
