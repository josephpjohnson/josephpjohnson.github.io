Research Summary

01 · Interacting Dark Sector

Field-Fluid Mappings & Coupled Quintessence

Investigating covariant energy-momentum transfer between dark energy scalar fields and cold dark matter, including disformal couplings and Horndeski theories, to test late-time cosmic acceleration and cosmological tensions.

02 · Modified Gravity & Tests of GR

Large-Scale Structure & Equivalence Principle

Deriving linear and quasi-linear cosmological perturbations in modified theories of gravity (such as f(R) and Ricci-inverse models) and testing Einstein's equivalence principle with next-generation VLBI observations.

03 · Model-Independent Cosmology

Gaussian Process Reconstruction & MCMC Inference

Reconstructing late-Universe expansion history and dark energy evolution without assuming parametric forms, and performing full Boltzmann-level Bayesian parameter inference using CMB, BAO, and supernova datasets.
